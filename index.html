01

About

I am a Master Student at ShanghaiTech University and got my B.S. degree from China University of Petroleum.

I am a Research Intern at the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University working with Prof. Yebin Liu from October 2025. I am also fortunate to collaborate with Prof. Hui Xiong at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, and Prof. Shaoliang Peng at Hunan University.

I am now working at Kling Team, Kuaishou Technology as a Intern from June 2026 on Video Generation. Before that, I worked at SignalPlus as a Intern from June 2025 to August 2025 on Multi-Agent System.

My research interests span 3D Vision and Multimodal, and their application on Multimodal LLM, Video Generation and Embodied AI. I actively seek collaborations and friends. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in any of these areas.
